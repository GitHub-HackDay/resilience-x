King County Emergency Response Report - Section 3.2

Cleanup operations in King County are experiencing significant delays due to critical crew shortages. Three major contractors (Pacific Northwest Cleanup LLC, Emerald Restoration Services, and Cascade Emergency Response) have reported staffing levels at only 60% capacity, severely impacting debris removal timelines across the region.

The shortages stem from multiple factors:
- Competing demands from neighboring counties also affected by the crisis
- Limited availability of specialized equipment operators
- Safety training requirements that prevent rapid scaling of crews
- Supply chain disruptions affecting equipment maintenance

Current debris removal capacity is estimated at 40% below pre-crisis planning assumptions. Priority areas include:
1. Critical infrastructure access routes (Highways, major arterials)  
2. Emergency service facility perimeters
3. Residential evacuation corridors

Recommended actions:
- Mobilize additional contractor resources from Oregon and California
- Expedite mutual aid agreements with unaffected neighboring jurisdictions
- Consider deploying National Guard engineering units for specialized debris removal
- Establish temporary debris processing sites to reduce transport bottlenecks

Status: Critical - immediate action required to prevent cascading delays in overall recovery timeline.
