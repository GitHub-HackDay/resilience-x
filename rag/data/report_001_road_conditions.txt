CRISIS REPORT 001: Road Conditions Assessment - King County
Date: March 15, 2024
Report ID: RC-2024-001

SUMMARY:
Major flooding has severely impacted transportation infrastructure across King County. Several key arterial roads remain impassable due to debris accumulation and standing water.

KEY FINDINGS:
- Interstate 405 northbound: Closed between exits 15-20 due to landslide debris
- SR-520 Bridge: Open but reduced to single lane, causing 2-hour delays
- Redmond-Woodinville Road: Completely blocked by fallen trees and power lines
- Bellevue Way SE: Flooded sections near Mercer Island, impassable to standard vehicles

RESOURCE CONSTRAINTS:
- Only 3 heavy clearing crews available (normal capacity: 8 crews)
- Equipment shortage: Missing 2 bulldozers for debris removal
- Estimated clearance time: 72-96 hours for major routes

PRIORITY ACTIONS:
1. Focus crews on I-405 debris removal (affects 50,000+ daily commuters)
2. Coordinate with utility companies for power line clearance
3. Request additional heavy equipment from neighboring counties
