CRISIS REPORT 003: Utility Service Disruptions
Date: March 15, 2024  
Report ID: UT-2024-003

SUMMARY:
Widespread power and water service disruptions continue across multiple service areas. Utility crews are working around the clock but face significant challenges.

POWER OUTAGES:
- Puget Sound Energy: 45,000 customers without power
  - Redmond area: 12,000 customers affected (Downtown, Rose Hill)  
  - Kirkland: 8,000 customers affected (Juanita, Totem Lake)
  - Woodinville: 25,000 customers affected (majority of city)

WATER SERVICE:
- City of Redmond: Main supply intact, localized outages in Rose Hill due to broken mains
- Kirkland Water Department: Boil water advisory for Juanita neighborhood  
- Woodinville: Water treatment facility damaged, emergency trucked water being distributed

RESTORATION TIMELINE:
- Power restoration: 24-72 hours depending on location
- Water service: Rose Hill repairs expected within 12 hours
- Woodinville water facility: 5-7 days for full restoration

CREW DEPLOYMENT:
- PSE has deployed 150 line crews (includes mutual aid from Oregon utilities)
- Water repair crews: 8 teams working on Rose Hill main breaks
- Bottleneck: Access to damaged areas blocked by fallen trees and debris on roads
