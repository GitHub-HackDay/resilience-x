Emergency Medical Response Coordination

Medical response during disasters requires rapid triage and resource allocation:

Immediate Response (0-4 hours):
- Establish incident command structure with medical operations chief
- Deploy search and rescue teams with medical personnel
- Set up temporary medical treatment areas near incident sites
- Activate mutual aid agreements with neighboring medical facilities

Triage Categories:
- Red: Immediate life-threatening injuries requiring urgent care
- Yellow: Delayed treatment, stable but requiring medical attention within 2-4 hours
- Green: Minor injuries, can wait several hours for treatment
- Black: Deceased or injuries incompatible with survival given available resources

Resource Management:
- Track available hospital beds regionally
- Coordinate ambulance services and medical transport
- Manage medical supply distribution from stockpiles
- Establish pharmaceutical distribution points for chronic medication needs

Medical facilities should maintain 72-hour operational capability with backup power, water, and medical supplies.
