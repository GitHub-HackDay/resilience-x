Shelter Management Operations

Emergency shelters provide temporary housing and essential services during disasters:

Shelter Setup Requirements:
- Minimum 20 square feet per person in sleeping areas
- Separate areas for families, single adults, and special needs populations
- Accessible facilities complying with ADA requirements
- Kitchen facilities or food service areas with health department approval

Staffing Structure:
- Shelter manager with emergency management experience
- Registration staff to track occupants and reunite families
- Security personnel to maintain order and control access
- Mental health counselors to provide psychological support
- Medical staff for basic healthcare needs

Operations:
- Maintain occupant registration logs with contact information
- Establish rules and schedules for meals, curfews, and activities
- Coordinate with social services for long-term housing assistance
- Provide information about disaster relief programs and resources
- Plan for pet accommodation in designated areas

Shelter operations typically continue for 2-4 weeks after major disasters, depending on available temporary and permanent housing options.
