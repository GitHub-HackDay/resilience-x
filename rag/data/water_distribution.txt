Emergency Water Distribution Guidelines

When municipal water systems fail during emergencies, alternate distribution methods become critical:

Distribution Points Setup:
- Locate sites with vehicle access for supply trucks
- Ensure 100 feet minimum distance from damaged buildings
- Establish separate lanes for pedestrians and vehicles
- Set up at schools, parks, or community centers when possible

Water Requirements:
- Minimum 1 gallon per person per day for drinking
- Additional 3 gallons per person per day for sanitation and cooking
- Priority distribution: hospitals, schools, elderly care facilities

Quality Control:
- Test all water sources before distribution
- Use only approved water containers and tanker trucks
- Maintain chain of custody documentation
- Post boil water notices if water quality is uncertain
