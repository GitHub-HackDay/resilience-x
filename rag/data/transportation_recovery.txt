Transportation System Recovery

Restoring transportation networks is critical for emergency response and community recovery:

Road System Priorities:
1. Primary evacuation routes and highways
2. Access roads to hospitals and emergency services
3. Routes to critical infrastructure (power plants, water treatment)
4. School bus routes and public transportation corridors

Bridge and Overpass Inspection:
- Visual inspection of all bridges over 20 feet in length
- Structural engineering assessment for bridges showing damage
- Load capacity evaluation before reopening to traffic
- Temporary bridge installation for critical connections if needed

Traffic Management:
- Deploy traffic control personnel at major intersections when signals fail
- Establish alternate routes around damaged areas
- Coordinate with fuel suppliers to maintain emergency vehicle operations
- Set up vehicle inspection points to prevent unauthorized access to damaged areas

Public Transit Recovery:
- Inspect rail lines, bus routes, and transit facilities
- Prioritize service to medical facilities and emergency shelters
- Provide free or reduced-cost transit during initial recovery period
