Building Damage Assessment Protocol

Following a natural disaster, building safety assessments must be conducted before allowing occupancy. The assessment process involves three phases:

1. Rapid Visual Screening: Initial 15-minute exterior inspection to identify obvious structural damage
2. Detailed Assessment: Comprehensive structural evaluation taking 2-4 hours per building
3. Engineering Evaluation: Required for buildings with significant damage, conducted by licensed structural engineers

Buildings are tagged with color-coded placards:
- Green: Safe for occupancy
- Yellow: Limited entry for essential items only
- Red: Unsafe, no entry permitted

Assessment teams should consist of qualified building inspectors or engineers with emergency response training. All assessments must be documented with photos and GPS coordinates.
