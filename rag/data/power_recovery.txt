Power Grid Recovery Procedures

Electrical system restoration follows a systematic approach to ensure safety and efficiency:

Assessment Phase (0-24 hours):
- Aerial and ground surveys of transmission lines, substations, and distribution systems
- Prioritization of critical infrastructure (hospitals, emergency services, water treatment)
- Damage documentation with GPS coordinates and photographic evidence
- Resource requirement estimation (materials, personnel, equipment)

Restoration Sequence:
1. Generation facilities and major transmission lines
2. Substations and primary distribution feeders
3. Secondary distribution lines to neighborhoods
4. Individual service connections

Safety Protocols:
- All downed power lines treated as energized
- Coordination with gas utilities before restoration
- Public safety announcements about electrical hazards
- Generator safety education for residents using backup power

Typical restoration timeline: 3-7 days for major outages, depending on damage severity and weather conditions.
