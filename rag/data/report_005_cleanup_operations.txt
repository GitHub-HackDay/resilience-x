CRISIS REPORT 005: Cleanup Operations Status
Date: March 16, 2024
Report ID: CLN-2024-005

SUMMARY:
Debris removal and cleanup operations are underway across the region. Progress is slower than anticipated due to resource constraints and access limitations.

DEBRIS ASSESSMENT:
- Estimated total debris volume: 15,000 cubic yards
- Tree debris: 60% of total (primarily in Woodinville and Redmond areas)  
- Building materials: 25% of total (roof damage, siding, fencing)
- Vehicles and equipment: 15% of total (abandoned or damaged vehicles)

CLEANUP PRIORITIES:
1. Critical transportation routes (I-405, SR-520)
2. Access to emergency facilities and shelters
3. Residential areas with blocked access
4. Commercial districts for economic recovery

RESOURCE ALLOCATION:
- Heavy equipment: 3 bulldozers, 5 dump trucks, 2 excavators active
- Personnel: 45 cleanup crew members across 6 teams
- Temporary debris storage: Marymoor Park designated as collection site

DELAYS AND CONSTRAINTS:
- Equipment shortage: Need 4 additional pieces of heavy machinery
- Access issues: 30% of affected areas still inaccessible due to road conditions  
- Personnel: Staff working extended shifts, fatigue becoming factor
- Weather: Rain forecasted for next 48 hours may slow outdoor operations

INTERAGENCY COORDINATION:
- Working with utility companies to coordinate infrastructure repairs
- Coordination with transportation department for road reopening priorities
- Environmental assessment required before debris disposal for contaminated materials
