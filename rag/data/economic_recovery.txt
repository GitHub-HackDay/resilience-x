Economic Recovery Planning

Economic recovery focuses on restoring business operations and employment:

Business Impact Assessment:
- Survey local businesses to determine damage levels and operational status
- Identify critical supply chain disruptions affecting multiple sectors
- Assess workforce displacement and housing impacts on employees
- Document economic losses for disaster declarations and federal assistance

Recovery Assistance Programs:
- Small Business Administration (SBA) disaster loans for businesses and homeowners
- Economic injury disaster loans for businesses unable to meet financial obligations
- Tax incentives and credits for businesses rebuilding in affected areas
- Workforce development programs for displaced workers

Long-term Recovery Strategies:
- Diversify local economy to increase resilience to future disasters
- Improve infrastructure to support business continuity
- Develop public-private partnerships for recovery projects
- Create business retention and attraction programs

Economic recovery typically takes 2-5 years depending on the severity of the disaster and the local economy's resilience. Tourism-dependent areas may require additional time and specialized recovery strategies.
