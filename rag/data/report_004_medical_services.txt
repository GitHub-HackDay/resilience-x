CRISIS REPORT 004: Medical Services and Healthcare Facilities
Date: March 16, 2024
Report ID: MED-2024-004

SUMMARY:
Regional healthcare facilities are managing increased patient loads while dealing with infrastructure challenges. Emergency medical services remain operational with modified protocols.

HOSPITAL STATUS:
- Overlake Medical Center (Bellevue): Operating on backup power, full capacity
- EvergreenHealth (Kirkland): Main power restored, accepting emergency transfers
- Redmond Medical Center: Limited operations due to water pressure issues

EMERGENCY SERVICES:
- Ambulance services: Response times increased 40% due to road closures
- Medic One units: 2 additional units deployed from Seattle to support region
- Alternative transport routes identified for critical cases

PATIENT IMPACTS:
- Dialysis patients: Temporary relocation to Overlake for Rose Hill residents  
- Elderly residents in Woodinville: Increased medical calls due to power outages
- Chronic condition patients: Medication access issues in isolated areas

SUPPLY CHAIN:
- Medical oxygen: Adequate supplies, delivery routes established
- Prescription medications: Pharmacies in affected areas operating on generator power
- Emergency medical supplies: Regional stockpile activated, supplies distributed

COORDINATION:
- Joint operations center established at Overlake Medical Center
- Direct communication link with emergency shelters for medical needs
- Volunteer medical personnel from Seattle area providing additional support
