Emergency Communication Systems

During crisis events, maintaining communication is essential for coordination and public safety:

Primary Communication Methods:
- Amateur radio networks (HAM radio) for emergency services coordination
- Emergency broadcast systems for public information
- Satellite phones for areas with damaged cellular infrastructure
- Social media monitoring for situational awareness

Communication Priorities:
1. Life safety communications (search and rescue, medical emergencies)
2. Infrastructure status updates (power, water, roads)
3. Public information and evacuation orders
4. Resource coordination between agencies

Backup Systems:
- Mobile cell towers can be deployed within 24-48 hours
- Mesh networks for local area communication
- Emergency messaging systems through local television and radio
- Door-to-door notification teams in affected neighborhoods
