CRISIS REPORT 002: Emergency Shelter Status - Regional Update  
Date: March 15, 2024
Report ID: ES-2024-002

SUMMARY:
Emergency shelters across the region are operating at 85% capacity. Several neighborhoods remain without power, driving increased shelter demand.

SHELTER LOCATIONS:
- Redmond Community Center: 150/200 capacity, adequate supplies
- Kirkland Recreation Complex: 180/180 capacity, FULL - no additional intake
- Bellevue High School: 95/150 capacity, requesting additional blankets
- Woodinville Senior Center: 45/60 capacity, suitable for families

AFFECTED NEIGHBORHOODS:
- Downtown Redmond: 800+ residents without power, many seeking shelter
- Rose Hill area: Water service disrupted, residents relocating temporarily  
- Crossroads District: Power restored, shelter demand decreasing

SUPPLY NEEDS:
- Medical supplies running low at Kirkland location
- Food service adequate for next 48 hours
- Transportation needed for elderly residents from Rose Hill area

COORDINATION ISSUES:
- Communication delays between shelters due to damaged phone lines
- Volunteer coordination hampered by staff shortages
- Need better system for tracking available capacity in real-time
