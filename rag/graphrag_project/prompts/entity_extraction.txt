-Goal-
Given a text document about crisis recovery operations, identify all entities mentioned and their relationships. Focus on locations, organizations, resources, incidents, and timeframes relevant to emergency response and recovery efforts.

-Steps-
1. Identify all entities mentioned in the text
2. For each entity, extract its type, description, and importance to crisis recovery
3. Identify relationships between entities
4. Rate the strength and importance of each relationship

-Entity Types-
* LOCATION: Geographic areas, neighborhoods, roads, facilities, shelters
* ORGANIZATION: Government agencies, utility companies, medical facilities, emergency services
* RESOURCE: Equipment, supplies, personnel, infrastructure, capacity
* INCIDENT: Specific crisis events, outages, damage, disruptions
* TIMEFRAME: Dates, durations, timelines, deadlines for recovery actions
