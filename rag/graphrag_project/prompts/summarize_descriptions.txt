-Goal-
Given a list of entities and relationships from crisis recovery documents, create a comprehensive summary that captures the key themes, critical issues, and recovery priorities. Focus on actionable insights for emergency response coordination.

-Steps-
1. Analyze all entities and their relationships within the crisis recovery context
2. Identify critical bottlenecks, resource constraints, and coordination challenges
3. Summarize recovery priorities and interdependencies
4. Highlight areas needing immediate attention or additional resources

-Focus Areas-
* Infrastructure status and repair priorities
* Resource availability and constraints  
* Population impacts and shelter needs
* Service restoration timelines and dependencies
* Coordination challenges between agencies
* Critical path items blocking recovery progress

-Format-
Provide a structured summary including:

**CRITICAL ISSUES:**
- List the most urgent problems identified
- Include impact assessment and affected areas

**RESOURCE CONSTRAINTS:**
- Personnel, equipment, and supply shortages
- Capacity limitations at facilities

**RECOVERY PRIORITIES:**
- Immediate actions (0-24 hours)
- Short-term goals (1-3 days)  
- Medium-term objectives (3-7 days)

**COORDINATION NEEDS:**
- Inter-agency dependencies
- Communication challenges
- Resource sharing requirements

**AFFECTED AREAS:**
- Geographic locations by impact severity
- Population displacement and service needs

-Examples-
**CRITICAL ISSUES:**
- I-405 closure affecting 50,000+ daily commuters due to landslide debris
- Kirkland shelters at full capacity with no additional intake capability

**RESOURCE CONSTRAINTS:**  
- Only 3 of 8 normal debris clearing crews available
- Missing 2 bulldozers for critical infrastructure repairs
